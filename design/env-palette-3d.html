ENVS terrain albedo — reference swatches (read-only)

Sampled from src/sim_core.js's ENVS.{urban,forest,ocean,dust}.col() for reference. These are anchor colors read out of noise-blended, per-branch procedural expressions (not standalone constants in the source) — extracting them into a real lookup table was scoped out of this pass: the color math is inseparable from the noise perturbation at each call site, and ENVS.col() runs per-splat during rebuild, so adding indirection there risked exactly the megorphic-property-access cost the project's own performance notes warn about. This page documents the palette for design purposes without touching the generator.
