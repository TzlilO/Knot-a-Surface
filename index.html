The idea, in three moves

Don’t fit a million Gaussians and hope a surface falls out. Start from the surface.

01

Optimise the surface

The learnable parameters are the control points and knots of spline charts. Thousands of control points — not millions of Gaussians.

Spline playground →
02

Derive the Gaussians

Position, orientation and size come from the surface’s own derivatives. Nothing is learned per Gaussian, so none can drift off its surface.

Calculus makes the splat →
03

Sample what the camera sees

A fixed Gaussian budget is re‑sampled over the (u, v) charts for every view. Detail on screen becomes a rendering decision — no retraining.
