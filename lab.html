What is real here

The objects are modelled by hand as B‑spline charts; the teapot is rebuilt from Newell’s 1975 Bézier patches. Nothing in this figure was reconstructed from photographs, and nothing was trained.

Each Gaussian is drawn as a flat disc in the tangent plane of its surface, with its position, orientation and size computed from the spline. The soft edges, the shadow and the occlusion are resolved by averaging frames while the view is still. This is a browser renderer, not the paper’s rasteriser.
